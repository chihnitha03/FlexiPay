How it works

  1. Sign in. The merchant backend signs a short-lived session for the logged-in customer, and the page embeds the widget.
  2. Gather. FlexPrice reads plans and usage history through the adapter. The merchant's data never moves.
  3. Decide. The forecaster, pricing engine and recommender compare every eligible plan over 12 months, and the policy engine applies the merchant's rules.
  4. Explain. Claude explains the result or answers questions using read-only tools. Any number not produced by the engine is rejected.
  5. Pay. The customer accepts the exact terms and pays with PayPal, or saves PayPal for usage billing. The server verifies the payment, asks the merchant to change the plan, then reads it back.
  6. Bill usage. The merchant streams usage events. At month end FlexPrice rates usage within the cap, notifies the customer, and charges the saved PayPal account.
Deterministic money, AI words.

Every price comes from the pricing engine and is computed in integer cents. Claude only explains results and calls read-only tools. A grounding check rejects any reply containing a dollar amount the engine didn't produce.

Payment you can trust.

Amounts are computed on the server and consent is bound to the exact terms. PayPal orders are captured and verified on the server, plan changes are idempotent, webhooks are verified and deduplicated, and reconciliation handles partial failures.

Fair retention.

When a customer moves to cancel, FlexPrice shows cheaper plans that fit their usage first. Discounts depend on merchant policy, with tenure, cooldown and lifetime limits, so clicking cancel never triggers one automatically.
